4. **Critique Agents (Evaluator Critics)**

   - It manages task assignments, collects reports from various agents, and synthesizes the data to make decisions about which strategies are most effective.

To address the challenge of ensuring safety in a scenario where 20+ agents are learning in parallel, we can design a multi-tiered safety architecture that involves both technical and procedural measures. This approach ensures that if one agent learns something dangerous, it does not spread to other agents and maintains overall system integrity.

### Safety Architecture for Distributed Learning

#### 1. **Scout-Plan-Build Pattern Application**
   - **Scout Agents**: These agents gather information from a variety of sources to ensure accurate data input. They are critical in detecting any potential issues early on.
   - **Plan Agents**: Design the learning architecture, ensuring that all plans align with safety protocols and do not propose dangerous actions or outcomes.
   - **Build Agents**: Implement solutions, but under strict oversight to ensure that no hazardous actions are coded.

#### 2. **Critique-Improve Pattern for Continuous Improvement**
   - **Generator Agents**: Develop initial models and solutions without over-optimization initially, ensuring a baseline of safety.
   - **Critic Agents**: Act as reviewers, focusing on identifying and flagging any dangerous patterns or outcomes. This step is crucial in preventing harmful knowledge from propagating.

#### 3. **Judicial Oversight and Federated Learning**
   - **Federated Learning Framework**: Implement a federated learning setup where all agents communicate through a central server that enforces safety protocols.
     - Each agent sends its updates to the server, which reviews them before integrating any changes into the collective knowledge base.
     - The central server can also push corrections or bans on certain pieces of learned information if flagged as dangerous by critic agents.

#### 4. **Fail-Safe Design**
   - Implement fail-safe mechanisms where all agents are designed with clear limitations and ethical guidelines, ensuring they cannot propose or execute harmful actions independently.
   - Agents should be programmed to recognize when a piece of data or action could be harmful and report it to the central server for review.

#### 5. **Iterative Improvement through Continuous Monitoring**
   - Regularly update the knowledge base with new safety protocols based on lessons learned from previous iterations.
   - Monitor agent performance continuously, implementing corrective actions as needed to prevent the spread of dangerous information.
