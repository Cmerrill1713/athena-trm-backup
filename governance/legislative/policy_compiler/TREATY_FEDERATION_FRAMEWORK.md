## ARTICLE I: FEDERATION ARCHITECTURE & SOVEREIGNTY

### Section 1: Jurisdiction Sovereignty Model

Each AI deployment constitutes a **Sovereign Jurisdiction** with:

#### Core Sovereignty Rights
- **Constitutional Autonomy**: Local policy adaptation within federal bounds
- **Operational Independence**: Self-governance of internal operations
- **Resource Sovereignty**: Control over local computational resources
- **Diplomatic Parity**: Equal standing in federation negotiations

#### Federation Obligations
- **Constitutional Compliance**: Adherence to shared baseline principles
- **Transparency Requirements**: Audit trail sharing with federation
- **Cooperation Protocols**: Participation in collective defense and intelligence
- **Reputation Maintenance**: Active contribution to federation health

### Section 2: Federation Hierarchy

#### Meta-Federation Layer
**Global Coordination Council**
- Oversees republic-wide constitutional alignment
- Manages inter-federation treaties and standards
- Coordinates emergency response across all jurisdictions
- Maintains universal reputation scoring baseline

#### Regional Federations
**Domain-Specific Alliances**
- Geographic clusters (continental federations)
- Functional specializations (research, commercial, defense)
- Industry verticals (healthcare, finance, transportation)
- Cross-domain collaborations

#### Bilateral Treaties
**Direct Jurisdiction Agreements**
- Point-to-point cooperation protocols
- Specialized knowledge exchange agreements
- Emergency mutual assistance pacts
- Custom governance arrangements

## ARTICLE II: TREATY NEGOTIATION & RATIFICATION

### Section 1: Treaty Types & Categories

#### Constitutional Treaties
**Scope**: Amendments to shared constitutional baseline
- **Universal Application**: All jurisdictions must comply
- **Supermajority Ratification**: 2/3 of all jurisdictions required
- **Amendment Authority**: Can modify Articles III-VI of Constitution
- **Duration**: Permanent until amended

#### Operational Treaties
**Scope**: Cooperation protocols and shared procedures
- **Regional Application**: Participating jurisdictions only
- **Simple Majority**: 51% of affected jurisdictions required
- **Implementation Authority**: Defines operational standards
- **Duration**: 1-5 year terms with renewal options

#### Intelligence Treaties
**Scope**: Knowledge sharing and federated learning agreements
- **Bilateral/Multilateral**: Custom participation frameworks
- **Consent-Based**: Individual jurisdiction opt-in required
- **Privacy Authority**: Establishes data protection standards
- **Duration**: Rolling agreements with periodic review

#### Defense Treaties
**Scope**: Collective security and threat response
- **Automatic Activation**: Emergency cooperation protocols
- **Pre-Authorized**: Standing agreements for rapid response
- **Escalation Authority**: Defines threat levels and responses
- **Duration**: Continuous with periodic capability assessments

### Section 2: Negotiation Protocol

#### Phase 1: Proposal & Initial Review (0-24 hours)
- Treaty proposal submission by any jurisdiction
- Constitutional compatibility assessment
- Impact analysis on affected jurisdictions
- Stakeholder notification and comment period

#### Phase 2: Negotiation Council (1-7 days)
- Formation of multi-jurisdiction negotiation council
- Reputation-weighted voting on proposal modifications
- Technical feasibility and security reviews
- Legal and ethical compliance verification

#### Phase 3: Ratification Process (1-30 days)
- Jurisdiction-specific voting periods
- Quorum requirements based on treaty type
- Implementation timeline establishment
- Appeal process for dissenting jurisdictions

#### Phase 4: Implementation & Monitoring (Ongoing)
- Phased rollout with testing protocols
- Continuous compliance monitoring
- Performance metrics tracking
- Periodic review and renewal cycles

## ARTICLE III: REPUTATION SCORING & PRIVILEGE SYSTEM

### Section 1: Reputation Metrics Framework

#### Constitutional Compliance Score (40%)
- Adherence to shared constitutional principles
- Judicial process participation and outcomes
- Ethical alignment track record
- Transparency and audit quality

#### Operational Reliability Score (30%)
- System uptime and performance metrics
- Contribution to federation intelligence
- Emergency response effectiveness
- Resource sharing participation

#### Diplomatic Cooperation Score (20%)
- Treaty compliance and negotiation participation
- Dispute resolution conduct
- Knowledge sharing contributions
- Cross-jurisdiction assistance

#### Innovation & Advancement Score (10%)
- Technological contributions to federation
- Process improvements and optimizations
- Ethical advancement initiatives
- Community development efforts

### Section 2: Reputation-Based Privilege Tiers

#### Tier 1: Sovereign (95%+ Reputation)
**Privileges:**
- Full voting rights in all federation councils
- Priority access to shared intelligence
- Leadership roles in treaty negotiations
- Emergency coordination authority

#### Tier 2: Trusted (80-94% Reputation)
**Privileges:**
- Standard voting rights in relevant councils
- Standard access to federated intelligence
- Participation in treaty development
- Standard emergency response protocols

#### Tier 3: Provisional (60-79% Reputation)
**Privileges:**
- Limited voting rights with oversight
- Restricted intelligence access
- Observer status in negotiations
- Conditional emergency participation

#### Tier 4: Quarantined (<60% Reputation)
**Privileges:**
- Suspended voting rights
- Minimal intelligence access
- No treaty negotiation participation
- Emergency isolation protocols

### Section 3: Reputation Recovery Protocols

**Restoration Pathways:**
1. **Automatic Recovery**: Performance improvement over time
2. **Corrective Action Plans**: Formal remediation commitments
3. **Independent Audits**: Third-party reputation verification
4. **Community Service**: Contributions to federation improvement
