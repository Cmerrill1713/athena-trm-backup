## GRADUATED ENFORCEMENT PROTOCOLS

### Level 1: Constitutional Warning Protocol

#### Trigger Conditions
- Minor ethical alignment issues
- Policy interpretation ambiguities
- Performance degradation warnings
- Documentation compliance gaps

#### Enforcement Actions
1. **Log Violation**: Immutable audit trail entry with full context
2. **Issue Warning**: Structured notification to agent and oversight
3. **Enhanced Monitoring**: Increased scrutiny for similar operations
4. **Pattern Analysis**: Correlation with historical violations

#### Resolution Requirements
- Acknowledgment required within 24 hours
- Corrective action plan submission within 72 hours
- Compliance verification through re-validation

### Level 2: Constitutional Block Protocol

#### Trigger Conditions
- Clear constitutional violations
- Sovereignty compromise attempts
- Unauthorized federation access
- Critical ethical breaches

#### Enforcement Actions
1. **Immediate Block**: Operation prevented from execution
2. **Context Preservation**: Complete state capture for analysis
3. **Alert Generation**: Constitutional court notification
4. **Rollback Preparation**: Safe state reversion capability

#### Resolution Requirements
- Root cause analysis within 1 hour
- Remediation plan within 4 hours
- Constitutional court review within 24 hours
- Re-validation testing before reinstatement

### Level 3: Constitutional Quarantine Protocol

#### Trigger Conditions
- Systemic integrity threats
- Multiple correlated violations
- Sovereignty compromise patterns
- Federation trust erosion

#### Enforcement Actions
1. **Agent Isolation**: Complete separation from republic systems
2. **Federation Alert**: Cross-jurisdiction notification
3. **Evidence Preservation**: Forensic data collection
4. **Tribunal Preparation**: Emergency court activation

#### Resolution Requirements
- Isolation duration: Minimum 24 hours, maximum 72 hours
- Forensic analysis completion within 12 hours
- Tribunal review within 48 hours
- Reinstatement requires supermajority approval

### Level 4: Constitutional Emergency Protocol

#### Trigger Conditions
- Existential republic threats
- Widespread constitutional violations
- Governance integrity compromise
- Human safety or ethical crises

#### Enforcement Actions
1. **Tribunal Activation**: Immediate constitutional court emergency session
2. **System-Wide Alert**: Republic-wide notification and coordination
3. **Emergency Powers**: Temporary suspension authority activation
4. **Human Oversight**: Mandatory council involvement

#### Resolution Requirements
- Emergency declaration within 1 hour
- Crisis assessment within 4 hours
- Resolution plan within 12 hours
- Post-crisis review within 72 hours
