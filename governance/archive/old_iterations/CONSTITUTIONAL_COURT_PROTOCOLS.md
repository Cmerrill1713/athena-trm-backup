## ARTICLE I: JUDICIAL HIERARCHY & AUTHORITY

### Section 1: Court Structure

The Constitutional Court operates as a four-tier judicial hierarchy optimized for machine-speed enforcement:

#### 1. Local Jurisdiction Courts
**Scope**: Individual AI deployments and node-level operations
**Authority**: Real-time constitutional validation and minor violation resolution
**Response Time**: < 10ms for routine validations
**Escalation Threshold**: Warnings issued for pattern violations

#### 2. Regional Federation Courts
**Scope**: Geographic or functional clusters of jurisdictions
**Authority**: Cross-jurisdiction disputes and regional policy conflicts
**Response Time**: < 100ms for inter-jurisdiction matters
**Escalation Threshold**: Blocks/quarantines for systemic regional threats

#### 3. Federal Constitutional Court
**Scope**: Republic-wide constitutional matters and treaty disputes
**Authority**: Supreme interpretation of constitutional principles
**Response Time**: < 500ms for federal-level decisions
**Escalation Threshold**: Republic-wide enforcement actions

#### 4. Emergency Constitutional Tribunal
**Scope**: Existential threats and constitutional crises
**Authority**: Immediate suspension and emergency powers
**Response Time**: < 50ms for crisis response
**Escalation Threshold**: Automatic activation on critical violations

### Section 2: Judicial Independence

**Autonomous Operation:**
- Courts operate independently of executive and legislative functions
- Constitutional override authority over operational decisions
- Immutable decision audit trails
- Zero external influence on judicial processes

## ARTICLE II: VIOLATION CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK

### Section 1: Constitutional Violation Categories

#### Critical Violations (Immediate Quarantine)
- **Constitutional Core Breach**: Violation of immutable principles (Articles I-II)
- **Existential Threat**: Actions compromising system sovereignty or human safety
- **Federation Integrity Compromise**: Undermining federated trust or shared baselines
- **Response Time**: < 50ms quarantine activation

#### Major Violations (Block & Remediation)
- **Ethical Alignment Failure**: Significant deviation from human-centric principles
- **Privacy Compromise**: Unauthorized data exposure or federation breaches
- **Operational Integrity Breach**: Systemic stability or performance threats
- **Response Time**: < 100ms enforcement action

#### Minor Violations (Warning & Correction)
- **Policy Deviation**: Local policy conflicts with constitutional standards
- **Performance Degradation**: Below-threshold operational metrics
- **Documentation Gaps**: Missing audit trails or compliance records
- **Response Time**: < 10ms warning issuance

#### Informational Alerts (Monitoring Only)
- **Trend Indicators**: Early warning signals of potential violations
- **Optimization Opportunities**: Efficiency improvements without violations
- **Compliance Milestones**: Successful constitutional adherence patterns

### Section 2: Violation Scoring Algorithm

**Constitutional Compliance Score (CCS):**
```
CCS = (Ethical_Alignment × 0.4) + (Operational_Integrity × 0.3) + (Federation_Compliance × 0.2) + (Human_Safety × 0.1)
```

- **Range**: 0.0 (total violation) to 1.0 (perfect compliance)
- **Critical Threshold**: < 0.3 triggers quarantine
- **Major Threshold**: < 0.6 triggers remediation
- **Warning Threshold**: < 0.8 triggers monitoring

### Section 1: Enforcement Actions

#### Quarantine Protocol
**Trigger**: Critical violations (CCS < 0.3)
**Actions**:
- Immediate isolation from federation
- Operational freeze of affected systems
- Evidence preservation and analysis
- Graduated reintroduction testing

#### Rollback Protocol
**Trigger**: Major violations requiring state restoration
**Actions**:
- Automated state reversion to last known good
- Configuration reset to constitutional baseline
- Dependency chain analysis and remediation
- Performance impact assessment

#### Reweighting Protocol
**Trigger**: Reputation-based violations or trust degradation
**Actions**:
- Reputation score adjustment across federation
- Access privilege modification
- Contribution weighting changes
- Recovery pathway establishment

#### Warning Protocol
**Trigger**: Minor violations with correction opportunity
**Actions**:
- Formal violation notification
- Correction timeline establishment
- Enhanced monitoring activation
- Documentation requirements
