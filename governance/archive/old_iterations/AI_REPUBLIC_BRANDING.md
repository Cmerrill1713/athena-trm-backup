## Usage Guidelines

### For Internal Documentation
1. **Always include version number** and effective date
2. **Use approved color palette** and typography
3. **Include table of contents** for documents >5 pages
4. **Add change history** and approval sections

### For External Communications
1. **Use executive summary** for leadership briefings
2. **Include logo and branding** on all materials
3. **Maintain professional tone** and terminology
4. **Reference constitutional principles** appropriately

### For Training Materials
1. **Use simple cheat sheet** for daily operations
2. **Include visual aids** and flowcharts
3. **Provide practical examples** and scenarios
4. **End with Q&A and contact information**
