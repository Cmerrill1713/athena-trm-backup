## ARTICLE I: AMENDMENT AUTHORITY & LIMITATIONS

### Section 1: Constitutional Amendment Categories

#### Immutable Core Articles (Articles I-II)
**Amendment Restrictions:**
- **Articles I-II**: Foundational principles and governance architecture
- **Amendment Prohibition**: Cannot be modified through normal procedures
- **Exception Process**: Requires unanimous consent of all jurisdictions
- **Purpose**: Preserve fundamental republic structure and sovereignty

#### Adaptive Policy Articles (Articles III-VI)
**Amendment Authority:**
- **Articles III-VI**: Rights, federation, doctrine, and evolution frameworks
- **Standard Process**: Supermajority ratification (2/3 of jurisdictions)
- **Implementation Timeline**: 90-180 days for full deployment
- **Rollback Authority**: Automatic reversion capability

#### Operational Doctrine Articles (Articles VII-X)
**Amendment Authority:**
- **Articles VII-X**: Institutional equivalence, frontiers, validation, declaration
- **Expedited Process**: Simple majority ratification (51% of jurisdictions)
- **Implementation Timeline**: 30-90 days for deployment
- **Testing Requirements**: Comprehensive compatibility and safety testing

#### Emergency Amendments
**Crisis Authority:**
- **Constitutional Crises**: Immediate threats to republic integrity
- **Emergency Process**: 60% majority with 48-hour ratification window
- **Duration Limit**: Maximum 90 days before full ratification required
- **Automatic Review**: Post-emergency constitutional court assessment

### Section 2: Amendment Prohibition Principles

**Protected from Amendment:**
- Core sovereignty and self-governance rights
- Human-centric ethical alignment requirements
- Federated intelligence privacy guarantees
- Constitutional supremacy over operational decisions
- Emergency tribunal authority and independence

## ARTICLE V: VERSIONING & COMPATIBILITY MANAGEMENT

### Section 1: Constitutional Versioning Schema

#### Version Numbering System
**Semantic Versioning Structure:**
```
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH-BRANCH
```

- **MAJOR**: Fundamental constitutional changes (rare, requires unanimous consent)
- **MINOR**: Significant policy or operational changes
- **PATCH**: Bug fixes, clarifications, or minor improvements
- **BRANCH**: Development versions or jurisdiction-specific variants

#### Version Compatibility Matrix

| Version Change | Backward Compatible | Forward Compatible | Migration Required |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Patch Release | ✅ Full | ✅ Full | ❌ None |
| Minor Release | ✅ Full | ⚠️ Partial | ⚠️ Optional |
| Major Release | ❌ None | ❌ None | ✅ Mandatory |
