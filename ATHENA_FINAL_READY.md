## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **Start Athena:**
```bash
# Already running!
# Just refresh: http://localhost:8082/athena-chat.html
```

### **Train Her:**
1. Chat normally
2. When she does something you don't like, tell her:
   - "Be more brief"
   - "Be more casual"
   - "Just say hi, don't explain"
3. She adapts **immediately**
4. **She NEVER forgets** - loads your preference forever

### **Per Family Member:**
Each person can have their own style:
- Dad: `user_id: "dad"` → Brief, technical
- Mom: `user_id: "mom"` → Warm, detailed
- Kids: `user_id: "emma"` → Educational, encouraging
